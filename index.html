Pillars of "Bangladesh 2.0"

Innovation

Bangladesh focuses on developing and adopting new technologies, such as AI-driven solutions and smart cities, to accelerate economic growth and enhance efficiency in industries.

Sustainability

The policy maker ensures long-term environmental and economic stability by prioritizing clean energy, green initiatives, and climate resilience to safeguard Bangladesh’s future.

Infrastructure

It involves large-scale development projects like metro rail, smart roads, and improved connectivity to strengthen the nation’s foundation for urbanization and industrial progress.

Empowerment

Bangladesh emphasises more on equipping individuals—especially youth and entrepreneurs—with the education, financial tools, and digital resources needed to drive inclusive growth and economic independence.

The Vision of Future

Bangladesh 2.0 aims to redefine the nation’s trajectory by embracing cutting-edge technology, promoting inclusive growth, and ensuring sustainability. With initiatives in renewable energy, digital transformation, and robust infrastructure, the country is set to become a global model of development.

Digital Transformation

Creating a nationwide digital ecosystem to enhance connectivity and access to services for all citizens.

Green Energy

Investments in solar and wind energy to ensure a sustainable and eco-friendly future.

Modern Infrastructure

Building state-of-the-art infrastructure to support economic growth and global competitiveness.
